# Mississippi Eats: name, icon and palette (final: Nick's decisions, 2026-11-07)

## Name
- **Mississippi Eats** (App Store name "Mississippi Eats: Restaurants", Nick's choice 2026-11-07, 29 characters; "Mississippi Eats: Restaurant Guide" is 34, over
  the 30 limit; Home Screen "Mississippi Eats", 16, which iOS may shorten under the icon to "Mississippi…"). A web search (2026-11-06)
  found no app called "Mississippi Eats"; near ones are the Mississippi Hospitality and Restaurant Association's "Mississippi Seafood
  Trail" app (2018), the state tourism app "Visit Mississippi" and the blog Eat Drink Mississippi. The prompt's App Store check
  (27 Sep 2026) found no exact match either.
- Alternatives from the prompt: **Mississippi Plates** (no App Store match); **Magnolia State Eats** (keep clear of the Gaines family's
  Magnolia brand, e.g. Magnolia Table; check their marks first).
- Avoided: "Birthplace of America's Music" (Visit Mississippi's tagline), "Hotty Toddy", "Hail State", "Egg Bowl" and school colours.
  The Home header's eyebrow is "THE MAGNOLIA STATE" (the state nickname).
- Subtitle: "Hot Tamales, Catfish & Po'boys" (30; the prompt's "Tamales, Catfish & Gulf Seafood" is 31, one over Apple's limit). The app's Home tagline keeps "Tamales, Catfish & Gulf Seafood".

## Icon (state-prompts/ICON-STYLE.md; all three pass check.swift; 60 px strip judged)
- **C, Nick's choice (2026-11-07): a curled Gulf shrimp with shell bands and a fanned tail, on flag navy.** It reads at 60 px as a shrimp,
  and the orange on navy is the strongest contrast of the three.
- A: three Delta hot tamales tied in their husks, on flag navy. The most Mississippi idea, but at 60 px the bundle can read as bread
  rolls or a parcel. The best alternative if Nick wants the Delta first.
- B: a cornmeal-fried catfish fillet with a lemon wedge, on cream. Passes, but the fillet reads as a generic fried food.
Drawn by `scripts/icon-concepts.swift` (PNGs in `playbook/brand/`); `scripts/make-brand.swift` makes the app icon, site icons and
docs/og.png from C. scripts/make-site.py's LOGO is the same mark as an SVG.

## Palette (the 2021 flag's; the Egg Bowl splits the state, so no school colours; as built, Nick's yes 2026-11-07)
Flag navy #00205B (15.5:1 on white) for titles and tint; flag red #BF0A30 (white on it 6.4:1) for the hand-checked guide cards; deep navy
#1B2F5E for honors chips (white 13:1); flag gold #C9A84C (nearer Pantone 7563 than the prompt's #C5B358) only as a fill under coal
#1E1E1E (8.0:1), never as text; cream #F6F1E4 pages. Header band: a row of magnolia blossoms (the state flower) in white with gold
centres on a flag-navy line; no flag star ring or motto. Light mode only.

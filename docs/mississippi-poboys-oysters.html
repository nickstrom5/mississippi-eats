Po'boys and Gulf oysters

6 places for po'boys and Gulf oysters, from Bozo's in Pascagoula to Bacchus on the Beach in Pass Christian. Each was checked in fall 2026.

The Coast and beyond

Visit Mississippi's guide to the state's favorite bites (updated 2026) sends po'boy eaters to Bozo's Seafood Market in Pascagoula, Fayard's in Ocean Springs and Biloxi, Fratesi's grocery in Leland and the Gold Post in Hattiesburg; its November 2025 bucket list adds chargrilled oysters at Bacchus on the Beach and Saltine's Gulf oysters in Jackson.

The list at a glance

One each in Biloxi, Hattiesburg, Jackson, Leland, Pascagoula and Pass Christian.

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (Visit Mississippi's own pages, the MICHELIN Guide or a dated local story) and its name matched a place in its town. No current source, and it stayed off. Review sites and inspection grades played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.

On your phone, Mississippi Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
